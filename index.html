About Bilal Broast

At Bilal Broast, we bring the authentic taste of Karachi's favorite fast food to your plate. Our broast is prepared using time-honored recipes, with crispy coating and juicy, tender meat inside.

Located in Jamshed Quarters Allama Binori Town, Karachi, we take pride in serving freshly prepared broast, burgers, and fast food that reflects the true essence of Pakistani fast food culture. Every dish is crafted with care, using quality ingredients and traditional cooking methods.

Whether you're dining with family or ordering for a gathering, our commitment to delicious flavors and warm hospitality ensures a memorable experience.
